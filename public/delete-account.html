Account & Data Deletion Request

Request full deletion of your SwiftHR mobile and web account, biometric vectors, session tokens, and personal records.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion Policy, users of the SwiftHR application can initiate a permanent account termination and data deletion request directly via email:

1

Send an Email from your Registered Account

Send an email to our official data protection desk at support.swifthr@gmail.com.

To: support.swifthr@gmail.com
Subject: SwiftHR Account & Data Deletion Request
Required Body: 1. Full Name
2. Registered Work Email Address
3. Employee ID (if applicable)
4. Employing Organization / Company Name
5. Statement: "I request permanent deletion of my SwiftHR account and associated personal data."
Open Pre-filled Email Request
2

Identity Verification & Employer Coordination

Our support team will verify your identity to prevent unauthorized deletion. If your account is tied to an enterprise organization, we will coordinate with your employer's designated administrator to confirm clearance.

3

Permanent Purging Timeline

Once confirmed, your account credentials, login tokens, biometric vectors, and personal identifiers will be permanently purged within 30 calendar days. A written confirmation email will be sent once completed.

Data Deletion Breakdown

In transparency with international privacy regulations and Google Play Developer guidelines, here is the scope of data handled upon account deletion:

● Permanently Deleted Data
  • User account login credentials & password hashes
  • Facial recognition mathematical feature vectors
  • Profile photos and personal media attachments
  • Device installation tokens and push session IDs
  • Active mobile application authorization tokens
● Statutory & Employer Retained Data
  • Historical work hours & payroll logs (required by statutory labor laws)
  • Tax and statutory financial compliance records
  • Archived audit logs retained under encrypted enterprise custody

Enterprise Note: SwiftHR operates as a software provider and Data Processor for your employer. Certain historical attendance and wage records are legally owned by your employing company to fulfill mandatory government labor regulations and tax audits, and are retained in encrypted, non-active archives per statutory timelines.
