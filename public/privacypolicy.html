Executive Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, transmission, and protection of personal and workplace information within the CreatonsHR mobile application (available on Google Play Store) and its associated web administrative suites (collectively referred to as the "Platform", "Service", or "Application").

CreatonsHR is a specialized enterprise Human Resource Management System (HRMS) engineered to facilitate workplace attendance verification, shift scheduling, leave administration, organizational communication, and workforce reporting.

Data Roles: Your employing organization is the Data Controller responsible for determining workforce policies and authorizing employee access. CreatonsHR acts strictly as the Data Processor, processing data in accordance with rigorous legal agreements, enterprise mandates, and international data privacy principles.

03

Location Data & Geofencing Transparency

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policy on Location Data Transparency, CreatonsHR enforces explicit safeguards regarding user location:

  • Foreground-Only Check-In Trigger: Location data is gathered exclusively at the precise moment an employee taps the "Clock-In" or "Clock-Out" button within the foreground application.
  • Zero Continuous Tracking: CreatonsHR does NOT continuously track your GPS coordinates in the background. Once the attendance event is processed, GPS acquisition immediately ceases.
  • Geofence Perimeter Calculation: Coordinates are compared mathematically against the employer's pre-configured office/project site boundaries to validate attendance eligibility.
  • No Location Commercialization: Location data is never shared, marketed, rented, or utilized for advertising or third-party telemetry under any circumstances.

User Choice: You may grant or revoke location permissions via your Android system settings at any time. However, if your employer enforces geofenced check-in policies, revoking location permissions may prevent completion of remote or on-site attendance marking.

Enterprise Security Architecture & Technical Safeguards

CreatonsHR employs an in-depth, defense-in-depth security framework built in alignment with internationally recognized cybersecurity standards (including ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 Type II control principles). All operations are governed by zero-trust architectural parameters:

Cryptographic In-Transit Protection

All communications between mobile clients, administrative consoles, and backend APIs are strictly encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS) and hardened cryptographic cipher suites.

Military-Grade Encryption at Rest

All persisted corporate data, employee profiles, timestamps, audit trails, and backup archives are encrypted at rest using 256-bit Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256) with automated key rotation.

Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation

Logical tenant partitioning ensures complete data segregation between organizations. Strict partition isolation tokens prevent any cross-tenant data visibility or unauthorized resource crossover.

Token Authentication & RBAC

Stateless cryptographically-signed authorization tokens with short expiration lifespans. Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) restricts administrative privileges strictly on a least-privilege basis.

Biometric Vector Protection

Facial attendance verification uses non-reversible, one-way mathematical feature representations (vectors). Raw photographic biometric templates are never stored or exposed across public endpoints.

Immutable Audit Logs & WAF

Comprehensive logging of authentication events, administrative modifications, and access attempts. Protected by Web Application Firewalls (WAF) and intelligent rate-limiting mitigators.

Additional Technical & Organizational Safeguards

  • Client-Side Storage Security: Mobile authentication credentials and refresh tokens are stored exclusively within hardware-backed secure storage containers (Android Keystore / Encrypted Storage). Cleartext secrets are never written to unencrypted shared storage.
  • Vulnerability Management: Continuous automated dependency scanning, code static analysis (SAST), and routine third-party penetration testing to identify and remediate potential attack vectors.
  • Disaster Recovery & Redundancy: Geo-redundant automated snapshots and continuous real-time replication guarantee 99.9% high availability and point-in-time disaster recovery.
  • Incident Response Protocol: In the event of a confirmed data security incident, CreatonsHR maintains an incident response protocol to notify affected enterprise controllers and regulatory authorities within 72 hours of verification.

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure Restrictions

CreatonsHR maintains an absolute policy against selling, monetizing, or trading personal or organizational workforce data.

Data disclosure is restricted strictly to authorized operational channels:

  • Your Employer (Authorized Enterprise Administrators): Your designated organizational administrators have access to attendance logs, leave balances, work hours, and profile details in accordance with corporate workplace governance.
  • Essential Infrastructure Sub-Processors: Secure cloud compute, encrypted storage, and transactional notification gateways operating under signed Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) requiring adherence to equivalent security and privacy standards.
  • Legal & Regulatory Mandates: Where disclosure is strictly compelled by lawful court order, subpoena, or enforceable governmental regulation.
08

Data Retention, Archival & Purging Policy

We retain personal and workplace data only for as long as necessary to satisfy operational purposes, contractual agreements with your employer, and applicable statutory retention requirements (such as labor laws, financial audit standards, and tax record mandates).

  • Active Employment Duration: Account records, shift schedules, and attendance histories remain active for the duration of the organization's subscription and your employment status.
  • Post-Termination Archival: Upon employee offboarding, records are transitioned into restricted read-only archival status pursuant to the employer's configured retention schedule (typically 3 to 7 years for statutory compliance).
  • Cryptographic Purging: When the statutory or contractual retention window expires, all associated data records, tokens, and visual vectors are securely purged using cryptographic overwriting routines.

User Privacy Rights & Regional Protections

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and national labor data privacy statutes), you possess distinct legal rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation of whether your personal data is being processed and obtain a portable copy of your records.
  • Right of Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete profile or attendance information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal records, subject to employer labor-record legal retention duties.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request temporary restriction of data processing during verification or dispute resolution.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain structured, machine-readable exports (such as CSV or JSON) of your attendance and profile records.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke mobile hardware permissions (Location, Camera) at any time via Android operating system settings.

Children's Privacy Protection

CreatonsHR is exclusively an enterprise workforce management tool designed for corporate employees and authorized contractors of legal working age. We do not knowingly market to, collect, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 16 (or under the minimum statutory working age in applicable jurisdictions).

If we become aware that personal information of a minor has been collected inadvertently without verified legal guardian or employer authorization, we will immediately delete the associated records from our systems.
